Ready to Adopt?

Adopting a cat is a big responsibility! but it is going to be a wonderful experience. At Adopt Me!, we want to help you find a cat that matches your home!

Before adopting please take some time to learn about our cats and understand all the adoption process.

Adoption Process

Step 1: Meet Our Cats

Please visit our Gallery page to meet the cats that are waiting for adoption. Read their stories and learn about their personalities

See Available Cats

Step 2: Choose a Cat

Once you find a cat that you are interested in, think about whether their personality and needs would be a good match for your home

Step 3: Contact Us

Fill out our contact form and let us know which cat you are interested in adopting. You can also ask any questions you may have.

Step 4: Meet Your New Friend

The next step is to arrange a meeting so you can spend some time with the cat and make sure you are a good match.

Step 5: Welcome Your Cat Home

Once the adoption is approved, you can prepare your home and welcome your new family member!

Adoption Requirements

Before adopting a cat, it is important to make sure you are ready for the responsibility.

  • Please be prepared to provide a safe and loving home.
  • Do you have enough time to care for your cat?
  • Provide food, fresh water, and a clean litter box.
  • Remember that you have to be able to afford regular veterinary care
  • Remember that adopting a cat is a longterm commitment

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know which cat is right for me?

Every cat has a different personality. Some cats are playful and energetic, while others are calm and enjoy relaxing. Do you know what personality fits you the best?

What do I need before bringing a cat home?

You have to have food, water bowls, a litter box, toys, and a comfortable place for your cat to sleep.

Can I adopt more than one cat?

Adopting more than one cat can be a good option if you have enough time, space, and resources to care for them!

Have questions about adoption?

You can visit our Contact page and send us a message with any questions you may have.
