Who We Are

Adopt Me! is a project created to help homeless cats find loving families. Many cats are abandoned or rescued from the streets and they desreve a second chance to have a safe and happy life.

Our website helps people learn about these cats, their personalities, and specially their stories before deciding to adopt them.

Our Mission

Our mission is to give homeless cats a better opportunity to find a permanent home. We believe that every cat deserves to be loved and cared for, no matter their age or history.

We also want to encourage responsible adoption and help people understnd the importance of taking care of their pets.

Why Adoption Matters?

There are many cats waiting for someone to give them a home. Some of them were abandoned, while others were born on the streets.

By choosing to adopt a cat, you are giving an animal a second chance and making space for other cats that need help.

Our Adoption Policies

We want every cat to find a safe and loving home. Before adopting, it is important to understand the responsibilities of having a pet.

  • Adopters should be prepared to provide food, water, and shelter.
  • Cats have to receive regular veterinary care.
  • Adopters must be ready for a longterm commitment.
  • Every cat should be treated with patience, love, and respect.
